Why "all serif" needs per-size tuning

Newsreader changes its drawing with optical size. The middle column lets the size pick the cut, which is what we would do per role. The right column pins the cut to 48, which is how the font is set up today. Compare the small rows.

Check the ₹ sign in the serif columns. If it looks like a fallback glyph, the bundled font lacks it and Android would fall back too.

Sans fonts

  • One UI Sans (Samsung) and Circular (Spotify, since replaced by Spotify Mix) are proprietary. They cannot be bundled in this app.
  • Google Sans Flex is the closest legitimate match to the Google Sans look. It was released under the SIL Open Font License in November 2025 (report), so bundling is allowed.
  • For the Circular feel, comparison sites name DM Sans, Figtree, Plus Jakarta Sans, Outfit and Manrope. Three of those are in the controls.

Colour notes

  • Money colours are unchanged. Brass sits near the warning amber, so the Unverified pill would need a different shape or label to stay distinct. Worth a look in dark mode.
  • Ivory and Graphite are the safest for contrast. Midnight and Aubergine look best in dark and are the most "private bank" of the four.
  • The glow is a soft radial tint behind the header, fading out before the hero number.

Type facts

  • Newsreader: optical size 6 to 72, weight 200 to 800, italic as a separate file (Google Fonts metadata).
  • Not confirmed: tabular figures in Newsreader, and the ₹ glyph.
  • Not confirmed: the exact axis list of Google Sans Flex. Sources disagree on five or six, and the app would use weight only.
