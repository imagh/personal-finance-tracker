Personal Finance Tracker: M1a visual direction (rough mockups)

For reaction, not pixel-final. "Cashew, but premium": quiet tonal surfaces, a single accent (dynamic in the app; ink indigo #4A55B8 here), inset grouped islands, and everything you tap sits in the lower part of the screen. The money colours are the plan's exact tokens. Every amount carries a sign or a label, so colour is never the only cue. All data is fake.

Header rule (owner, 2026-10-29): titles are sans, read-only and centred in the upper band of the header. The serif is used only for the dashboard hero number. The hero, its caption and anything tappable (‹ › period stepper) sit low, in the thumb zone. Expense rows are neutral with "−"; bottom navigation is Home · Budgets · Upcoming · More.
